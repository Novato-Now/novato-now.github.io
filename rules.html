How We Connect:
Community Rules

Connecting streets, fostering local trust.

ZoneUp was built on the foundation of neighborhood trust. A street thrives when its residents feel safe, welcome, and connected to one another.

These community rules are here to protect your safety, safeguard your personal privacy, and keep our local groups clean, neighborly, and constructive. Read on to discover how we build a healthy street culture.

"To foster safe, neighborly connections, we design guidelines that help you meet people who live right next door, share local advice, and respect each other's privacy."

What We Mean by "Healthy Neighbors"

Every profile on ZoneUp represents a real home on your street or neighborhood. Because of this real-world correlation, the way we engage online translates directly into how we interact when walking down our driveway or visiting a local park. A healthy neighbor is someone who participates transparently, respect others' boundaries, and values community development.

This means keeping discussions civil, avoiding targeted harassment, and reporting suspicious activities. We verify address locations to ensure no bad actors can register under false location credentials, preserving ZoneUp as a pure, verified local ecosystem.

How Profile Verification Works

Trust is established by ensuring authenticity. Every neighbor undergoes a structured location validation process before gaining posting rights inside local boards.

Once verified, members receive a verified check badge next to their profile. This lets neighbors interact with confidence, knowing they are talking to real neighborhood residents, not anonymous bots or external spammers.

Who Can See Your Profile?

We respect your boundaries. Unlike traditional networks, ZoneUp restrict profile visibility to your local grid radius. External users or neighboring sectors cannot browse your posts or street location.

You retain absolute control over how much of your profile is visible to others, and we never expose exact map pin coordinates to protect your home safety.

Our Six Pillars of Conduct

Help us keep ZoneUp a positive and supportive space by practicing these fundamental guidelines.

1

Be Respectful

Treat neighbors with kindness. Personal insults, discrimination, hate speech, and harassment are strictly prohibited.

2

Verify to Connect

Keep your account authenticated. Address verification ensures a safe, transparent environment for everyone on the street.

3

No Commercial Spam

Do not flood the main community feeds with repetitive commercial ads or business promotions. Use designated spaces.

4

Share Helpful Info

Post items to lend, organize community events, alert neighbors of local safety issues, and share trusted reviews.

5

Protect Personal Privacy

Never post private contact information, exact house coordinates, or candid photos of neighbors without explicit consent.

6

Report & Flag Concerns

Actively help maintain board health. Report policy violations, spammers, or safety issues immediately to our moderation team.

How Activity & Feedback Impact Our Board

We believe local governance is the best way to moderate neighborhood discussion boards. Content flagging, reporting metrics, and community upvotes help surface the most relevant local recommendations while burying unconstructive comments.

Our volunteer neighborhood guides review flagged content based on these core community pillars. By working collaboratively, we preserve a positive space where neighbors can coordinate help during emergencies, locate lost pets, and support local growth.

"Building trust starts on your street, but scales when we collectively practice kindness, respect, and local helpfulness."

Looking Ahead

As ZoneUp expands to support more cities and neighborhoods, we will continue to adapt our community rules to meet new safety and moderation demands.

We are committed to building collaborative features that empower local streets to govern themselves transparently, ensuring that neighborhood trust scales side-by-side with our technology stack.
