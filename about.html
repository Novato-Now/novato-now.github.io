About Us

Connecting neighborhoods,
building friendships.

ZoneUp is a local platform built on the belief that knowing the people around you makes life richer and safer. We connect verified neighbors, foster community discussions, and turn nearby updates into real-world connections.

Verified by Design

We believe everyone should feel safe talking to neighbors. That's why we verify address details to make sure ZoneUp remains a space for real neighbors only.

Building for the Community

From organizing neighborhood cleanups to finding walking buddies or sharing local recommendations, we build features that bring people together.

Privacy as a Priority

Your location details are kept secure. We design features to connect you without compromising your personal space or data safety.

Inclusive and Open

A neighborhood thrives when everyone is included. ZoneUp is open to all, helping foster relationships across generations and local interests.

Founders' Note

A letter from our founders

Every neighborhood has a story, but as our cities grow, we've noticed that neighbors are becoming more distant. We started ZoneUp with a simple mission: to use technology to bring back the warmth of local community.

We wanted a safe, private, and trusted space where you can share local recommendations, help out a neighbor, keep up with nearby updates, and turn online interactions into real-world friendships.

We believe that a connected neighborhood is a safer, friendlier, and happier place to live. Thank you for joining us on this journey to bring neighborhoods closer together.

Sarah Jenkins Co-Founder, ZoneUp
Dr. Marcus Chen Co-Founder, ZoneUp
