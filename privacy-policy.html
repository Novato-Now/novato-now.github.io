Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction and who we are

The ZoneUp app is operated by ZoneUp, Trichy, Tamil Nadu (“ZoneUp”, “we”, “us”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the ZoneUp mobile application (the “App”), how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

This policy applies to the App and related services we provide. It does not apply to third-party services or websites you reach through the App; please read their privacy policies before sharing information with them. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms and Conditions.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

• Account details: name, date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or older), username, password. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the basic details you allow them to share, such as your name and email address.

• Profile details: gender, language and interests.

• Public profile: your username, name, profile picture and any community pictures you upload are visible to all ZoneUp users.

• Content: posts, comments, photos, videos and other media you share, communities you create or join, and reports you submit.

• Messages: messages you send in direct messages (DMs) and communities. Section 5 explains how these are protected and stored.

2.2 Information we collect automatically

• Location: your device location, if you give permission (see section 4).

• Device information: IP address, device type, operating system and device identifiers.

• Usage information: features you use, screens you view, and how often and how long you use the App.

• Local storage: the App stores some data on your device (for example, settings, stickers, GIFs and cached media) so it works faster.

• Stickers and GIFs: these are loaded directly from third-party providers such as Giphy. When you view them, your device's IP address is sent to that provider. They are not stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted.

3. How we use your information

• To run the App: create and manage your account, show you posts and people nearby, and deliver messages.

• To personalise: show content based on your area, language and interests.

• For safety: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms, prevent fraud and abuse, and protect users.

• To communicate with you: send service and security messages about your account. We send promotional messages only with your consent.

• To improve the App: understand how features are used and fix problems.

• For legal reasons: comply with the law, respond to lawful requests from authorities, and enforce our Terms.

4. Location

ZoneUp is built around where you are, so location is central to how it works. With your permission, we use your device's location to place you in a zone and show you posts, communities and people nearby.

• Other users see only your approximate area or zone, never your exact location.

• Posts you share to the Nearby feed are public. They can be seen by anyone using ZoneUp, including people outside your area.

• We use your device's exact location (latitude and longitude) only to work out which zone you are in. We store your zone, not your exact location.

• You can turn off location access at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work without it.

5. Messages and encryption

DMs (one-to-one chats): text messages and media in DMs are end-to-end encrypted, so in normal operation only the sender and recipient can read them. DM media (photos, videos, voice notes and files) is kept in encrypted form on our servers for up to one year, then permanently deleted. Save anything important to your device.

Communities: text messages in communities are not end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted while travelling between your device and our servers, and we relay them to community members without keeping them on our servers after delivery. If you forward media from a DM to a community, that copy is no longer end-to-end encrypted, and we store it so community members can see it.

Reported content: when a message or post is reported, we keep a copy so we can review it and act on it.

No system is completely secure. Bugs, device vulnerabilities or unauthorised access can affect encryption. We take strong measures to protect your messages but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads or share your information with advertisers.

We share information only with:

• Other users: your public profile, and the posts and community content you share. Remember that anyone can see Nearby posts.

• Service providers: companies that help us run the App, such as cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, and media providers (e.g., Google Cloud, Firebase, Cloudflare, Giphy). They may use your information only to provide their services to us and must keep it confidential.

• Law enforcement and authorities: when required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a government agency, or to protect someone's safety.

• Business transfers: if ZoneUp's business is transferred to a company or another entity (for example, when we incorporate as a private limited company), your information will move to that entity, which will remain bound by this Privacy Policy.

7. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored on servers located in India and secure cloud regions. Some of our service providers may process information in other countries. Where that happens, we take steps to make sure your information stays protected and is handled as Indian law requires.

8. Security

We use industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and end-to-end encryption for DMs. No internet service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will inform you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. How long we keep information

Information How long we keep it
Account and profile While your account is active
Posts and comments Until you delete them or your account
Burn posts Hidden from users after the set time; permanently purged from our servers automatically
DM media Up to 1 year, then permanently deleted
Community text messages Not kept after delivery
Media forwarded to a community Until the community or the post is deleted
Reported content Until the report is resolved, and longer where the law requires

When you delete your account:

• Your profile and content are removed from public view immediately.

• Your data is kept in secure backups for 30 days so you can recover your account if you change your mind.

• After 30 days, it is permanently deleted, except information we must keep by law. Under Indian law, we may keep registration details and information about removed content for 180 days or longer, and share it with authorities when lawfully required.

10. Your rights and choices

• See and update your information: most of it is in your profile and settings in the App.

• Correct your information: update it in the App, or contact us if you can't.

• Delete your account: from Settings in the App, or at zoneup.in/delete-account.html.

• Withdraw consent: turn off location in your device settings, opt out of promotional messages, or delete your account. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

• Complain: contact our Grievance Officer (section 13). If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children

ZoneUp is only for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth to check this, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it and their information.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version in the App and on our website and update the “Last updated” date. For important changes, we will notify you in the App and, where required, ask you to accept them before you continue.

13. Grievance Officer and contact

For questions, requests or complaints about your privacy or this policy, contact:

Grievance Officer: Grievance Officer, ZoneUp
Email: support@zoneup.in

We will acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours and resolve it within the time required by law.
